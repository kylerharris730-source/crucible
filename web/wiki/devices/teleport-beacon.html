Teleport Beacon

A machine, 14×14 cells.

Its setting

Adjustable
no — this one has nothing to tune

Beacons link by name. Right-click a new beacon to name it, and give a second beacon the same name. Right-clicking either one then puts you on top of the other. Exactly two beacons may share a name: a third with that name breaks the link until one is renamed or mined. Names are up to 11 characters and ignore case. Shift+right-click renames a linked beacon. Each name shows above its beacon, purple while it is linked and grey while it is not.
