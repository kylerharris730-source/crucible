# Farming and eating

Wheat exists so that bread exists. Before bread there was a loop with nothing in
the middle: you could grow wheat, harvest it, and turn it back into seed.

## You will need

- Seeds — [wheat](../materials/wheat-seed.html),
  [flax](../materials/flax-seed.html) and
  [cotton](../materials/cotton-seed.html) all come from their grown plant, 1
  plant to 2 seeds, by hand
- Exposed soil, and light
- A [Harvesting Sickle](../items/harvesting-sickle.html) is worth making

## Steps

1. **Find or make exposed dirt.** Grass spreads onto it; crops want it bare.
2. **Sow the seed.**
3. **Wait.** Plants grow where they have soil, light and water.
4. **Cut it with a sickle.** The sickle cuts only what grew, so it clears a
   field without eating the floor under it.
5. **Make [Bread](../items/bread.html)** — it restores 30 health.

## When it works

You have a field you can come back to and a stack of bread. All consumable
healing shares one cooldown, so bread is a between-fights food rather than a
mid-fight one.

## When it does not

**One seed behaves differently from all the others**, and it is worth knowing
which way round.

Almost every seed is a **material**: [oak](../materials/oak-seed.html),
[birch](../materials/birch-seed.html), [wheat](../materials/wheat-seed.html),
[flax](../materials/flax-seed.html), [cotton](../materials/cotton-seed.html)
and [flower](../materials/flower-seed.html). You place or scatter them, they
fall, and they germinate where they settle on soil with air above. Aim at the
ground.

[Grass Seed](../items/grass-seed.html) is the exception. It is an item of a
different kind that **converts a cell** rather than becoming one, so you have to
aim at the strip of **empty air directly above** soil, not at the soil itself.
Aiming it at the ground does nothing at all, silently.

If sowing grass feels like it "does not place right", that is why, and it is the
verb rather than anything you are doing wrong.

**Nothing grows.** Check all three of soil, light and water. A crop in a dark
cellar will sit there.

**Your field burned.** Every crop on this page is flammable and most of them
catch below 100 °C — [cotton](../materials/cotton.html) at 60 °C is the lowest
ignition point in the game. Do not farm next to your furnace.

**You mined the crop instead of cutting it and got nothing useful.** Use the
sickle.

**You want the seed back out of a plant.** Most plants render back to two seeds
by hand — see the [Hand](../recipes/hand.html) recipes.
