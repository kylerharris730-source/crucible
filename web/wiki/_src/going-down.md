# Going down

The underground is three layers, and they are sealed from each other. You do not
drift from one into the next: there is a floor, it is called
[Stratum](../materials/stratum.html), and no tool in the game breaks it.

## You will need

- Light, always — see [The dark is not scenery](the-dark.html)
- Gear appropriate to the layer, which is what
  [Fighting back](fighting.html) and [Gearing up](gearing-up.html) are for

## Steps

1. **Layer 1** runs from the stone line down about 2,100 cells. Copper, tin,
   coal, iron. The creatures here are the ones you meet first, and the layer's
   boss drops the [Forge Core](../items/forge-core.html) that becomes your
   Blast Furnace.
2. **Layer 2** runs from there to about 4,100 cells down. Hotter, and the
   creatures hit harder.
3. **Layer 3** is everything below that. All four of its creatures are heat
   tolerant well past the default, because the layer itself runs hot enough to
   kill something that is not.

Each layer's creatures are listed on the
[Creatures](../creatures/index.html) index, with a "Found" column that says
which layers each one spawns in.

## When it works

You arrive in a new layer with light, a way to fight, and something to carry ore
home in — rather than arriving by falling and discovering all three are missing.

## When it does not

**You have hit a grey floor you cannot dig.** That is Stratum, and it is
supposed to stop you. It sits above every tool in the game by design — hardness
235, where the strongest thing you can swing reaches 210. The way down is not
through it.

**The heat is killing you.** Layer 3 runs hot. Check what you are wearing: heat
protection is a real stat on armour, and each item's page states its number in
degrees.

**Things hit much harder than they did one layer up.** They do. Compare health
and contact damage on the [creature index](../creatures/index.html), sorted by
either column — the step between layers is visible as a number.

**You fell a long way and died.** Rope helps. So does not digging straight down,
which is the thing everyone does once.

**You cannot find the ore you came for.** Ore is layer-dependent. Titanium and
tungsten are deep, and they also need [coke](coke-retort.html) to smelt once you
have them — check you can actually use a thing before you go a long way for it.
