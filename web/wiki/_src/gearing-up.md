# Gearing up

There is one rule here that is counter-intuitive, that the game never states,
and that costs people real material when they get it wrong.

**Bonuses are not added up. The largest single one counts.**

Two cheap reach trinkets do not beat one good one — they do nothing at all
beyond the better of the two. Wearing a second pair of speed boots buys you
exactly zero.

## You will need

- Whatever you are choosing between. Every item's page states its slot and its
  bonus.

## Steps

1. **Learn the slots.** Head, Body, Feet, Back, and six interchangeable Trinket
   slots. The six trinkets are genuinely interchangeable — nothing cares which
   one a given trinket goes in.
2. **Pick the biggest bonus in each category, not the most items.** Reach and
   ground speed both resolve to the single largest you are wearing.
3. **Fill the trinket slots with *different* effects.** Six slots is an
   invitation to combine, not to stack.
4. **Wear both halves of an armour set.** This is the exception.

## When it works

**The exception, and why it exists:** armour *is* summed. A helmet and a suit
add together, and so does accessory armour — once per item.

That is not an inconsistency. The largest-never-summed rule exists to stop two
cheap items beating one good one, and it bites where several slots can hold the
same *kind* of thing — two trinkets, boots against a jetpack. Worn armour cannot
do that: a helmet only fits Head and a suit only Body. There is no slot to stuff
and no ladder to shortcut, and a game where wearing both protects you exactly as
much as wearing one is a game that has taught you not to bother with the helmet.

**Feet is a real decision.** Rocket boots get you *up*, Hermes boots get you
*along* at +60% ground speed, both go on the feet, and you cannot have both.
That contest is why the slot exists.

## When it does not

**You put on a second trinket and nothing changed.** Correct, if it duplicated
an effect you already had a better version of. Check the numbers on both pages
— only the larger is doing anything.

**You wore two of the same accessory.** Duplicate accessories never stack, even
for armour.

**A jetpack cost you your boots.** It should not — packs go on the Back and
boots on the Feet, deliberately, so that combining them is something crafting
can reward.

**You cannot find where a trinket goes.** Any trinket slot. The item page says
"Trinket" rather than naming one, because naming one would be a distinction the
game does not make.

**Heat or cold is still hurting you through armour.** Protection is in degrees
and it is a threshold, not immunity. The number is on the item's page; compare
it to where you are standing.
