# Bees and wax

Six of the seven shot modifiers want wax, and the first thing to know is which
wax.

**[Beeswax](../materials/beeswax.html) is not [Wax](../materials/wax.html).**
They are different materials with different pages. The recipes want beeswax. A
stack of wax will not do, and hunting for the wrong one is a long way to go for
nothing.

## You will need

- A wild hive, or a hive you built
- Somewhere to put what comes out

## Steps

1. **Find a wild hive.** Worldgen places **three**, one per third of the map,
   each in a carved bowl. They exist so that the supply is there *before* you
   have made anything — building a hive is not something you can do before you
   have any wax to want.
2. **Take the beeswax.** A hive extrudes it.
3. **Use it at the bench.** The modifier recipes are on the station pages, and
   [Beeswax](../materials/beeswax.html) lists every one of them under "What it
   is for".

## When it works

You have beeswax, and the modifier recipes stop being greyed out.

## When it does not

**You have wax and the recipe still will not light up.** Check which wax. This
is the one mistake this page exists to prevent — the first attempt at this whole
feature used the wrong material, and six recipes went from asking for something
you cannot keep to asking for something that does not exist.

**You soured the colony with coal.** Coal near a hive gives
[Coal Wax](../materials/coal-wax.html) and
[Coal Honey](../materials/coal-honey.html) instead. This is recoverable: both
render back into coal at the bench, so a soured hive is a detour rather than a
loss.

**You wanted to catch a bee.** You cannot, and that is deliberate rather than
missing. Right-click capture was built and removed: right-click is also the dig
verb, so every swing near a hive pocketed a bee. The items still exist and still
release a bee if you somehow have one.

**You are looking for [web](../materials/web.html) for a recipe.** Nothing in
the crafting table asks for web any more. It decays with a mean life of about
255 frames — roughly four seconds — which made collecting it a minigame nobody
asked for. Silk is a pure hazard now, and the modifiers are made of beeswax
instead.
