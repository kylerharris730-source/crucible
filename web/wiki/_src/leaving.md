# Leaving

**Spoilers.** This page is the end of the game. If you would rather find it,
stop here.

---

You get off the planet. That is what the game is called for, and it is a thing
you build.

## You will need

- A [Launch Assembly](../items/launch-assembly.html), which places the rocket
- Fuel, and a lot of it
- A crew

## Steps

1. **Build the Launch Assembly** and place it somewhere with room. The rocket is
   28 cells wide and 80 tall, and it needs a clear corridor above it — it will
   refuse to launch under a ceiling.
2. **Stand it on solid ground.** It needs support under its feet; dirt and sand
   count as well as stone.
3. **Fuel it.** It wants a substantial quantity, loaded in.
4. **Fit the core.**
5. **Gather the crew** — they have to be nearby when you light it.
6. **Light it**, and wait out the countdown.
7. **Watch it go.**

## When it works

The countdown runs, the rocket lifts, and you get the ending.

Then it offers you **Continue Exploring**, which puts the crew back on the pad
with the world and your gear exactly as they were. Finishing the game does not
take the world away from you.

## When it does not

**It refuses to launch and will not say why.** It will say why — the assembly
reports its fault. The usual ones are below.

**"No corridor."** Something is above it. The rocket needs a clear column all
the way up, and a ceiling you forgot about counts.

**"No support."** It is not standing on anything solid. Static blocks and
powders both work; it cannot launch out of a hole with air under its feet.

**Not enough fuel.** It takes a real quantity, and the assembly counts what is
loaded rather than what is in your pack.

**The crew are not aboard.** They have to be within range when the countdown
starts, not merely alive somewhere on the map.

**You wanted a save before the ending.** The world is handed back intact
afterwards, so this is less final than it looks.
