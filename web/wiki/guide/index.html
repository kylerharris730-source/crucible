Guide

Guides to the game's tools, materials, and systems.

In order

Start at the top if you're new to the game.

  1. Your first ten minutes: Move, dig, and mine.
  2. Lighting dark areas: Use light to stop creatures spawning nearby.
  3. Making fire: Start a fire and keep it under control.
  4. Digging: Choose a tool for the ground you need to clear.
  5. Crafting stations: Build the stations that unlock new recipes.
  6. Ore into bars: Match ore with a fuel that burns hot enough.
  7. Making coke: Heat fuel in a sealed retort to make coke.
  8. Going down: Prepare for the layers below the surface.
  9. Fighting back: Choose weapons and modules for each fight.
  10. Gearing up: Equip armour, accessories, and tools.
  11. Farming and eating: Grow crops and make food.
  12. Bees and wax: Find a hive and collect beeswax.
  13. Circuits: Connect devices and use their signals.
  14. Item logistics: Move items between devices with pipes.
  15. Leaving: The end of the game. Spoilers.

More guides

  • How heat behaves: Understand conduction, thermal mass, and temperature.
