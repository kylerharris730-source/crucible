The Void

Find the post-launch portals, survive the descent, and defeat the Unseen Mother.

The Void is a postgame dimension unlocked by launching the rocket. It is a separate, saved map with no sunlight, a long descent, five kinds of creature and the Unseen Mother at the bottom. Its enemies combine fast pursuit, pounces and projectile fans. Bring your best equipment before entering.

Finding a Void portal

After launch, a black Void portal appears near the surface at each far end of the overworld. Travel to either world edge and touch the portal to enter. Both lead to the same Void map. Your overworld, buildings and machines remain saved, and you take your current equipment, inventory and health with you.

You arrive on a shelf near the top. The entrance closes behind you. Climbing back to the arrival shelf does not take you home: you must defeat the boss below or die to leave.

Before you enter

  • Bring torches or a carried light. There is no daylight or skylight. Torches and lanterns keep their normal reach and brightness. Explored terrain stays faintly visible, but a light is still needed to read the route.
  • Bring healing and upgraded weapons. Ordinary Void creatures deal 32-40 contact damage before your defences. Arriving with low health makes the first encounter dangerous.
  • Equip mobility gear. Air jumps and movement speed help with broken shelves, retreating from a pounce and avoiding projectile fans.
  • Plan for the whole trip. Beds cannot be placed here. Trying displays "No beds in the Void" and does not consume the bed.

Following the descent

A twisting central passage connects fossil-lined side chambers. Broken shelves alternate across the passage, with silk pockets along the walls. Use the shelves as landings and light the next section before dropping. The Void HUD shows how far you have descended.

Keep working downward toward the broad bottom chamber. Side chambers give you room to move, but the route home is through the boss arena. Terrain changes and placed equipment persist when you save or return later.

The creatures and their trinkets

Every ordinary Void creature drops Ichor and has a 1-in-12 chance to drop its own trinket. Repeated encounters can therefore reward the journey even before you defeat the boss.

  • Grave Skitter: A fast, eight-legged pursuer. It crouches before pouncing and has about five seconds of recovery between attacks. Move away from the spot it aimed at. Its Widowglass Fang gives +24% damage and +4 flat damage.
  • Pale Harvester: A larger spider with a heavier leap and about seven seconds of recovery. Use its wind-up to create space. Grave Strides gives +28% movement speed and one extra air jump.
  • Blind Lantern: A floating, tentacled creature that keeps its distance and locks its aim before firing three bolts. Change direction after the warning. The Blind Lantern Lens gives +30% projectile speed and 16% shorter firing delay.
  • Silk Weaver: A ground spider that fires a wider three-bolt fan. Avoid standing still during its wind-up. The Black Silk Knot gives +14% movement speed; sustained movement builds up to another +35% damage over 2.5 seconds.
  • Hollow Revenant: A close aerial pursuer with trailing tentacles. Keep room to dodge rather than letting it pin you against a wall. Hollow Pulse gives +18% damage and another +30% while below half health.

The Unseen Mother

The Unseen Mother waits in the bottom arena. She has 11,000 health before multiplayer scaling, fires locked-aim projectile fans and summons pairs of Hollow Revenants. Her first pair arrives no sooner than twenty seconds into the fight, with at least twenty seconds between later waves. Summons do not speed up below half health.

Move after she commits her aim and clear the summons before they crowd your escape route. Below half health, her fans widen from five bolts to nine and she fires more often. Keep enough space to change direction.

Defeating her guarantees two trophies:

  • Heart of the Void: +35% damage, +20% movement speed and +2 weapon recharge.
  • Crown of the Unseen: +25% movement speed, two extra air jumps and 18% shorter firing delay.

Returning home and revisiting

The Mother's defeat opens a pale return portal in the arena. Collect your trophies, then touch it to return. If it appeared underneath you, step clear and enter it again. The portal stays open on later visits; unclaimed boss trophies wait in the arena across reloads and visits.

Dying also returns you to the overworld, where the normal respawn delay and your remembered bed apply. Collected Void loot comes home with you. You can save and reload inside the Void without losing the separate map.

Multiplayer: Everyone shares the active dimension. Entering a portal, using the return portal or dying in the Void moves the whole connected party. Prepare together before anyone crosses.
