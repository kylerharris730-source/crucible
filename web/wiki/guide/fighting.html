Fighting back

Choose weapons and modules for each fight.

A multitool needs modules before it can deal damage. The tool holds and fires them; the modules determine what each shot does.

Your starting Bolt Caster does 4 damage. A Shot Module does 7, so even a basic multitool gives you a stronger shot.

Build a weapon

  1. Make a Multitool Mk I at the bench from 4 Copper and 2 Wood. It holds three modules. Mk II holds five and fires faster.
  2. Make and install at least one module. A Shot Module costs 2 Copper and 1 Wood. A Blast Module does 24 damage.
  3. Put the modules in the order you want them to work. Shot modules fire from left to right. A modifier changes the spell in front of it; it does not fire on its own.

If the tool does nothing, open it and check for an installed damage module. Upgrading the chassis gives you more slots and firing speed, while damage comes from the modules. A Mk I with a shot module will serve you better than an empty Mk II. The Items index can be sorted by Damage to compare modules.

A multitool also uses charge. Mk I holds 120 and recovers 1 per frame, and each shot spends some. The tool and module pages list their numbers.

Some creatures keep their distance and shoot. Their pages list that behaviour and their shot damage. A Blast Module can help against them. Bosses appear when summoned, so choose a place to fight before calling one.

Armour reduces damage from each hit. A helmet and suit add their armour together; Gearing up explains how equipment bonuses work.
