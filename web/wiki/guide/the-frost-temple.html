The frost temple

The frozen east, a tower to climb, and the Hoarfrost Wyrm at the top.

East of the familiar lands, past the high flats, the ground turns white. That is the cold region, as wide as the desert on the other side of the world, and in the middle of it stands the frost temple: a stepped tower you climb rather than cross, with a reward on each floor and the Hoarfrost Wyrm asleep at the top.

This page covers the cold, the climb, and waking what lives up there. The boss half is a spoiler, and it is marked.

Before you go

  • It is cold enough to hurt. The cold region's surface and shallow rock sit at -10 °C, which is just past the point where cold starts costing you health. Out in it without protection you are slowly frozen. Cold resistance answers it, and one good piece is enough: iron armour or anything better cancels it outright, as does the Wolf Pelt that Frost Wolves sometimes drop. Pieces do not add up -- the best one you wear is what counts.
  • Water freezes. Anything wet you bring or dig into freezes to Ice. Plan any water you need to be somewhere warm.
  • The ground is Snow. It is the cold region's soil. Dig it and you get nothing; warm it and it melts into water, which in the cold freezes to ice rather than back to snow. You cannot carry it or make it -- only the world makes snow.
  • It has its own creatures. The Frost Wolf runs you down on the snow, the Ice Wraith drifts through walls toward you and fires a beam, and the Snow Harpy climbs above you and dives. They live only in the cold, and they are tougher than their cousins elsewhere.

Getting in

Walk east until the snow starts, and keep going. The temple stands on a graded base with a doorway at ground level on each side.

The climb

The temple is five floors stacked on top of each other, each narrower than the one below, with a spire on top. Every floor but the last is a hall with a staircase of ledges up one side, each ledge a single jump above the last, climbing to a hatch in the ceiling.

Each floor's staircase is on the opposite side from the one below. So every time you come up through a hatch, you cross the whole hall to find the next way up -- and the creatures in it have a chance at you while you do.

Ice Wraiths and Snow Harpies are the ones to watch in here. A wraith does not care about walls, and a harpy dives on you from above, which is exactly where a tower keeps putting it.

The three treasures

There is one reward on each of the second, third and fourth floors, against the wall on the side you climb in -- turn around when you come up through the hatch. Right-click a pedestal to take what is on it.

  1. Second floor: a Culverin Loader: some of your shots come out doubled.
  2. Third floor: a Slagmaw Gullet: an unloaded tool's shots leave fire where they land.
  3. Fourth floor: a Cinderling Ash: you leave a trail of hot embers as you run -- careful what you run past.

All three are charms you would otherwise have to hunt for deep underground, one kill in fifty at a time.

The Frost Engine

The top floor is one open chamber under the spire. At its centre stands the Frost Engine: the same shrine as the pyramid's, in silver, with a pale moon-disc turning above it. A shaft of cold light rises from it, and it lights the room around it. Walk up close and a small mouse icon appears above the disc, with its right button lit.

Right-click the Frost Engine to summon the Hoarfrost Wyrm. There is no item to craft and nothing is used up. Only one Wyrm can be awake at a time. The shaft of light goes out and the disc turns violet while it is.

The Hoarfrost Wyrm — spoilers

The Hoarfrost Wyrm is a flying serpent: a horned head, and a long body of segments that follow it, each carrying a crystal on its back. The body curls wherever the head has been.

The body is the armour

While any segment lives, the head takes only a quarter of what you deal it. The health bar shows a pip for each segment, and the word ARMOURED while any are lit. Kill a segment and the chain closes up behind it. The body is also the easiest thing to hit -- it is most of the Wyrm, and it is always somewhere near you.

What it does

  • Frost breath. It hangs off to one side of you, its jaw opens, and dotted lines show the cone. Then it sprays frost down that cone. Get out of the cone, or behind the altar.
  • Dash. It rears back away from you and a dotted line shows its lane. Then it lunges down the lane at full speed. Step off the line.
  • Icicles. It rises. Icicles form on the ceiling above where you stand, with dotted lines under them, and then they fall. Move out from under them.
  • Shard ripple. The crystal on every segment lights up, one after another, and each fires out of both sides of the body in turn, head to tail. Stay out of the body's line.
  • Coil. It circles you, close, so its body is a ring around you, and spits bolts inward as it goes. Break out through a gap, or kill a segment and make one.

It never uses the same attack twice in a row.

When it burns

Once most of its body is gone, or its health falls low enough, its crystals and eyes turn violet. From then on it moves faster, dashes twice -- straight back at you after the first -- and gains a nova: it rises, a ring closes in on it, and it fires two staggered rings of shards in every direction.

The reward

Beating it drops the Frost Sigil, and the game records the win. You can wake it again from the Frost Engine whenever you like. If everyone goes a long way from it, it leaves, body and all. That is not a loss and not a win.
