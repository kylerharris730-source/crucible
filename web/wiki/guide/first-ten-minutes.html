Your first ten minutes

Move, dig, and mine.

You start on the surface with a Bolt Caster, your starting weapon. Your pack also has wood, a Flint Striker, a Warp Wand, and a Field Guide. Select the guide and left-click to open it, or press F1 at any time.

In the first ten minutes, get light, make a fire, place a workbench, and make a bed.

Get started

  1. Right-click to dig; left-click to build. Right-click a tree trunk when you need more wood. If clicking does nothing, click the game window first so it has your mouse and keyboard input, especially if you are playing in a browser.
  2. Dig through the soil into stone. Collect some Stone and look for a dark seam of Coal. Coal is common in the first few hundred cells below the surface. If you are still on grass, keep digging. Sand and dirt can fall into a hole, so tunnel sideways through stone instead of digging straight down under a beach.
  3. Press C to open crafting. Tab opens your inventory, where you can check your pack, equipment, and tool modules. If you enabled cheats in the pause menu, the inventory also lists every item for free.
  4. Make torches. 4 Wood + 1 Coal makes 4 torches. Make several and place them as you dig. Creatures spawn in unlit areas, including places you have already passed. See Lighting dark areas.
  5. Start a fire. You have a Flint Striker in your pack. Put some wood or coal in a safe spot and strike it. You can make another striker from 4 Stone if you lose yours. See Making fire for fuel and fire safety.
  6. Make a Workbench. It costs 4 Wood + 2 Stone. Place it somewhere you will return to. Stand near it to use its recipes. If the crafting list is short or greyed out, check whether the recipe needs a station. The Hand page lists the 16 recipes you can make without one.
  7. Make a bed. It costs 6 Wood + 6 Flax. Flax grows on the surface.

Once the workbench is down, you can start making more tools and stations. The crafting stations guide shows what each one lets you make.
