Gearing up

Equip armour, accessories, and tools.

For bonuses such as reach and ground speed, only the highest equipped value counts. Two reach trinkets will not add their bonuses together. Check each item's page for its slot and bonus before you spend materials on another one.

Equipment slots

You have a Head, Body, Feet, and Back slot, plus six interchangeable Trinket slots. A trinket can go in any of the six. Use them for different effects instead of filling them with copies of the same bonus.

The Feet slot makes you choose between movement items. Rocket boots help you go up, while Hermes boots give +60% ground speed. You cannot wear both. A jetpack goes in the Back slot, so you can use it with boots.

Armour and protection

Armour adds together across equipped items. Wear both a helmet and a suit: each reduces the damage you take from a hit. Armour from accessories also counts once per item, but duplicate accessories do not stack, even when they give armour.

Heat and cold protection are measured in degrees. They raise the temperature you can tolerate; they do not make you immune. Compare the protection listed on your equipment with the temperature where you are standing.

If a new trinket changes nothing, compare its bonus with what you already wear. For reach and ground speed, the larger value is the one that applies.
