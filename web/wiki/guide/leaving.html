Leaving

The end of the game. Spoilers.

Spoilers. This page covers the end of the game. Stop here if you want to find it yourself.

To leave the planet, build and launch a rocket.

Building the rocket

Make a Launch Assembly and place it on solid ground. The rocket it creates is 28 cells wide and 80 cells tall. Leave a clear corridor above it all the way to the sky. Dirt, sand and stone all provide support under its feet.

Load plenty of fuel into the assembly, fit the core and bring the crew close to the rocket. They must be nearby when you start the countdown. Light it, then wait for launch.

If the rocket will not launch, check the fault reported by the assembly:

  • No corridor: Clear anything above the rocket, including ceilings far overhead.
  • No support: Put solid blocks or powder under its feet.
  • Not enough fuel: Load more into the assembly. Fuel in your pack does not count.
  • Crew not aboard: Bring the crew into range before starting the countdown.

After the ending, Continue Exploring returns the crew to the pad with the world and your gear intact.
