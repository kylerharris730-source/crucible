Bees and wax

Find a hive and collect beeswax.

Six of the seven shot modifiers use Beeswax. Check the name before crafting: Wax is a different material and will not work in those recipes.

Finding beeswax

The world starts with three wild hives, one in each third of the map. Look for the carved bowls that hold them. Hives extrude beeswax, which you can collect and use at the bench. The Beeswax page lists the recipes under "What it is for".

You can also build a hive later. If coal gets near a hive, it makes Coal Wax and Coal Honey instead. Both can be rendered back into coal at the bench.

You cannot catch a bee with right-click. Bee items still exist and release a bee if you have one, but there is no way to capture one.

Web is not needed for any crafting recipe. It decays after about 255 frames on average, roughly four seconds. Silk is a hazard; the shot modifiers use beeswax.
