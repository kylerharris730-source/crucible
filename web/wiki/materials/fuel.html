Fuel

Liquid — Coal touching Steam makes Fuel. For Coke, seal it behind stone and heat the Fuel to 115 C with a wood fire outside. The Flint Striker stops at 110 C; wood ember reaches 130 C, below Fuel's 135 C ignition point. Keep embers from touching Fuel; a gas sieve can vent larger batches.
