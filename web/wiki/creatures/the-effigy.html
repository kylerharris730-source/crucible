The body alternates locked floor fissures with a stomp. Leave the amber footprints before they ignite. Upward arrows over a low lane warn of the stomp: jump over its travelling waves or use solid cover. Each wave deals 28 damage. The stomp gives 1.1 seconds of warning. After each body attack, pale green brackets mark an open cage: it stops, cannot deal contact damage, and takes full damage even with parts attached. Killing all parts removes its armour permanently and adds wider fissures and a warned fire fan. The arms, crown and body take turns attacking.
